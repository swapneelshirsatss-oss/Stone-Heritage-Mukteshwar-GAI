## High-Altitude Pet Care & Safety Guide for Mukteshwar

If this is your dog's first time visiting an altitude of 7,500 feet in the Himalayas, a little preparation ensures a completely smooth journey:

### 1. Managing Mountain Road Motion Sickness
The winding mountain ascent from Kathgodam / Haldwani up to Mukteshwar (approx. 62 km) takes about 2 to 2.5 hours. 
- **Feed early:** Avoid giving your dog a heavy meal right before starting the hill climb.
- **Fresh airflow:** Keep a window cracked open slightly so your dog can smell the cool mountain air.
- **Scenic pit stops:** Take a 10-minute break at Bhimtal or Bhowali to let your dog stretch. See our detailed [Delhi to Mukteshwar Road Trip Itinerary](/blog/delhi-to-mukteshwar-road-trip/) for recommended stops.

### 2. Hydration & Altitude Adjustment
High-altitude air is crisp, fresh, and noticeably drier. Ensure your pet has constant access to fresh, clean drinking water throughout the day. Keep hydration bowls filled in your room and on the garden terrace.

### 3. Nighttime Temperature Control
Mountain temperatures drop significantly after sunset, even in summer. While our thick stone walls retain ambient warmth, bring your pet's favorite blanket or fleece jacket so they feel snug and cozy at night.

### 4. Forest Leash Etiquette
While our private lawns are safe, always keep your dog on a leash when exploring deep forest trails or hiking near cliff views like [Chauli Ki Jali](/blog/top-5-things-to-do-in-mukteshwar/). The forest is home to local wildlife, birds, and steep terrain where a leash ensures complete safety.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. Are all pet breeds allowed at Stone Heritage Mukteshwar?
Yes! We welcome dogs of all sizes and breeds, as well as cats. We simply request that pets are house-trained, friendly, and kept on a leash in shared public areas out of courtesy to other guests.

### 2. Is there an extra fee for bringing a pet?
We charge a nominal, transparent pet cleaning fee to ensure rooms are thoroughly sanitized and deep-cleaned for subsequent guests. Contact us directly via WhatsApp/phone for exact details when booking.

### 3. Can I get custom food prepared for my dog at the homestay?
Absolutely. Our kitchen can prepare boiled chicken, rice, eggs, or plain broth for your dog. Please let us know in advance or at check-in so our team can have it ready.

### 4. Is the property fenced and safe for dogs?
Stone Heritage features gated private lawns and garden boundaries. However, because we are located on the edge of nature trails in Mukteshwar, we recommend keeping dogs supervised when outdoors.

### 5. What medical facilities are available for pets near Mukteshwar?
Basic veterinary clinics are available in Bhowali (approx. 30 km away), while comprehensive veterinary hospitals are located in Haldwani / Kathgodam (approx. 60 km away). Our host team can assist with local contact numbers if needed.
