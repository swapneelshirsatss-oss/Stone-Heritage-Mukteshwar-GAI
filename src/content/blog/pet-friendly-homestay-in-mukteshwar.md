## Top 5 Things to Do Near Mukteshwar with Your Pet & Family

Using [Stone Heritage Mukteshwar](/experiences/) as your basecamp puts you within 20 to 25 minutes of Mukteshwar's most iconic natural landmarks while keeping you out of town congestion:

### 1. Bhalu Gaad Waterfall Forest Trek (24 Mins / 9.5 km)
A scenic 1.5 km forest trail winding through pine and oak trees down to a 60-foot horseshoe waterfall. The shallow stream crossings and shaded trails make it one of the most rewarding nature walks in Kumaon for dogs.

![Bhalu Gaad Waterfall nature trail near Mukteshwar ideal for family hikes and dogs](/images/experiences/Bhalugaad-Waterfall-Mukteshwar.webp)

### 2. Mukteshwar Dham Temple (20 Mins / 8.2 km)
A sacred 350-year-old Lord Shiva temple perched atop the highest ridge in Mukteshwar at 7,500 feet. It offers profound spiritual tranquility and expansive vistas of the snow-clad Himalayas.

![Mukteshwar Dham Temple on the highest ridge in Mukteshwar Uttarakhand](/images/experiences/Uttarakhand-Nainital-Mukteshwar-Temple.webp)

### 3. Chauli Ki Jali Cliff & Sunset Point (21 Mins / 8.5 km)
Dramatic overhanging natural cliff rocks right beside Mukteshwar Dham, famous for panoramic sunset photography, valley views, and Himalayan raptor sightings. Keep your dog safely leashed near cliff edges.

![Chauli Ki Jali cliff rocks overlooking the valley near Mukteshwar](/images/experiences/chauli-ki-jali-in-mukteshwar.webp)

### 4. Dhanachuli Apple Orchards & Village Walks (15 Mins / 6 km)
Tranquil terraced apple, peach, and plum orchards line the peaceful village bends of Dhanachuli, perfect for serene afternoon walks with your pet.

### 5. Neem Karoli Baba Ashram — Kainchi Dham (1 Hr 22 Mins / 38 km)
Start early at 6:30 AM from Stone Heritage for a smooth, scenic morning drive down the Bhowali corridor to visit Kainchi Dham Ashram before holiday crowds arrive.

![Kainchi Dham Ashram of Neem Karoli Baba near Bhowali Nainital](/images/experiences/Kainchi_dham_mukteshwar.webp)
